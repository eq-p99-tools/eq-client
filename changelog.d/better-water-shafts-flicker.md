- Better Water: light shafts under water now fall through the world from
  the sun, bent at the surface, so they slant with the sun, stay put as the
  camera turns, slide past while swimming and end on the floor and walls;
  none in zones without a sky. Water surfaces no longer flicker as the
  camera moves (each side of the surface was drawn twice), reflections
  slide instead of popping, and far glints no longer sparkle on and off.
  The offline viewer's `--start-heading` now faces the camera that way.
