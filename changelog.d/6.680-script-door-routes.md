- Scripted walks can join nearby mesh islands through modeled usable doors,
  after checking each direction with the ordinary collision walk. They
  approach and use the door, waiting for its collider to clear before moving
  through. A refusal removes the connection; walls and missing ground stay
  blocked. Ordinary Titanium sliding passages now use their drawn completion
  for the same wait as hinged doors. Lifts and unknown mechanisms stay blocked.
